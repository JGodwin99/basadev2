Connect Employees to Meaningful Impact

Staff fundraising is one of the most practical ways to connect employees to meaningful impact. It turns small, voluntary contributions into collective action supporting real solutions in communities while strengthening team culture. At BASA Development Initiatives (BDI), we work with companies to design staff fundraising initiatives that are simple to manage, engaging for employees, and clearly linked to results on the ground in Liberia.

Where Your Support Goes

Recovery Pathways

Supporting young people affected by drug use with recovery and reintegration.

Girls in School

Ensuring access to menstrual products so girls stay in school and graduate.

Agribusiness Growth

Helping women and youth build sustainable income through agriculture.

Community Health

Delivering based solutions in livelihoods, health, and local resilience.

How It Works

We keep the process straightforward and flexible based on what works for your organization:

Payroll Giving

Small monthly contributions through a structured system.

Workplace Campaigns

Short, focused fundraising drives around a specific goal.

Team Challenges

Staff-led events that bring teams together.

Matching Contributions

Companies match donations to double the impact.

What BDI Provides

Clear campaign materials & messaging
Defined use of funds for specific programs
Impact stories connecting your team to the change
Transparent financial reporting
