Honor a Life Through Lasting Impact

“Transforming loss into purpose and remembrance into impact.”

A Living Legacy

Giving in memory goes beyond a symbolic gesture. It creates a living legacy.

A memorial gift is a meaningful way to honor the life of someone special while supporting work that continues to make a difference. It allows family, friends, colleagues, and communities to turn remembrance into action by helping others through practical, life-changing programs.

At BASA Development Initiatives (BDI), donations made in memory support locally led work that strengthens lives and communities across Liberia and Africa. It is a thoughtful way to ensure that a loved one’s memory is linked to dignity, hope, and lasting change.

How Your Tribute Supports the Future

Youth Pathways

Helping vulnerable young people find dignity and recovery from addiction.

Girls' Education

Supporting girls to stay in school by providing essential menstrual health resources.

Agribusiness Growth

Creating sustainable economic opportunities for women and rural youth.

Community Resilience

Building health and livelihood systems that sustain communities for the long term.

Commemorate a Loved One

Individuals and families can choose to make a one-time or recurring donation. In many cases, families request memorial donations in place of flowers.

We can provide guidance to ensure your tribute is organized clearly and respectfully.
