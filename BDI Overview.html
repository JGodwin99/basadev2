Our Founding Story

Born from a belief that Liberians deserve better, and can build it themselves.

When Katherina M. Hopkins founded BASA Development Initiatives in 2022, she did so with a simple but unshakeable conviction: Liberians have the capacity, the creativity, and the courage to build the country they deserve. What they often lack are the resources, the systems, and the partners who truly believe in them.

BDI began with agriculture and the environment - training farmers, planting trees, running conservation campaigns across Liberian counties. But the needs were too interconnected to stay in one lane. Today, BDI works across eight thematic program areas, from health and fisheries to disability inclusion, all united by a single goal: a Liberia without poverty.

What Guides Us

Eight values that shape everything we do.

From the way we design programs to the way we treat our partners and communities, BDI's core values are not words on a wall. They are the standards we hold ourselves to every single day.

Integrity

Honesty, transparency, and accountability in every relationship and decision we make.

Empowerment

Fostering self-reliance so communities take charge of their own development journey.

Inclusiveness

Every voice heard, especially the most marginalized - because no one gets left behind.

Love for People

A genuine passion for humanity and a deep desire to improve living conditions for all Liberians.

Sustainability

Practices ensuring long-term environmental, social, and economic viability for future generations.

Collaboration

Strong partnerships and collective action, because lasting impact is never achieved alone.

Innovation

Creative thinking and bold, evidence-based solutions to complex development challenges.

Professionalism

The highest standards in all activities, representing our communities and country with dignity.

Where We Work

Rooted in Liberia. Focused on the communities that need us most.

BDI works across all 15 Liberian counties, with a particular focus on the Southeast region - the country's least developed and most underserved area. Three offices keep us close to the communities we serve.

Headquarters - Monrovia, Montserrado County

Coordinates national and international partnerships, institutional management, and strategy across all eight program areas.

Southeast Office - Greenville, Sinoe County

Serves Liberia's least developed region with direct programming in agriculture, fisheries, environment, and community development.

Satellite Office - Bong County

Provides central and northern Liberia coverage, supporting program delivery and community engagement in the region.
