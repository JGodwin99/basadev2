Our Team Culture

A team bound by more than job descriptions.

Every person on BDI's team was hired not just for their skills, but for their alignment with BDI's values and their genuine commitment to Liberia. We work hard, we learn constantly, we hold each other accountable, and we celebrate every milestone - however small - because we know what it means for the communities behind each number.

Mission First

Every decision is weighed against one question: does this serve the communities we exist for?

Evidence Over Assumptions

We collect data, study what it says, and change course when the evidence demands it.

Grow Together

BDI invests in its staff because organizational impact is only as strong as individual capacity.

Community is Why We Are Here

The people we serve are not our target group. They are the reason every single one of us shows up.

Want to join a team building a Liberia without poverty?

BDI is a growing organization with ambitions that match Liberia's needs. We periodically recruit for program, finance, communications, and field-based roles. If you are passionate about community-led development and want to be part of something meaningful, we would love to hear from you.
