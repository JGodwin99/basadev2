Donate Now

Find a Friend to Sponsor — One Person. Real Impact.

Sometimes, the most powerful support starts with a simple decision: one person choosing to stand behind another.

Tangible Commitment

Sponsorship is not just a donation. It is a commitment to a real journey.

Connect to a specific cause or individual pathway, turning giving into something tangible and meaningful.

Recovery Journey

Supporting youth transitioning from drug use back into education or work.

Stay in School

Providing a girl with the menstrual support she needs to stay in class consistently.

Agri-Empowerment

Supporting a woman or youth as they build a small, sustainable agribusiness.

Stability Support

Helping a vulnerable individual regain their stability through structured aid.

Simple & Transparent

How It Works

1
Connect with BDI

Reach out to identify a sponsorship profile.

2
Select Focus

Choose the type of support you want to provide.

3
Define Level

Set the amount and duration of your support.

4
Start Journey

Your contribution goes to the agreed intervention.

5
Receive Updates

Get periodic reports on progress and impact.

Find a Friend to Sponsor

Invite someone else to give. Encourage a friend, colleague, or family member to sponsor alongside you. When more people commit, more lives are supported.

Accountability

What BDI Provides

We ensure that every sponsorship is purposeful and well-managed through our verified program frameworks.

  • Clear Structure: Defined use of funds for every individual.
  • Verified Needs: All profiles are part of our core impact programs.
  • Regular Progress: Ongoing updates on milestones and outcomes.
  • Strong Accountability: Detailed financial management and reporting.
