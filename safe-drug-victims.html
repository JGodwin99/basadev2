Urgent Crisis Response

Help Us Respond to Liberia's Growing Drug Crisis

A quiet crisis is deepening. Kush and other substances are claiming a generation. We are stepping in to provide a path back.

Rising Surge

Drug use among youth under 30 is at an all-time high.

System Gap

Functional rehab centers are overstretched and scarce.

Lack of Support

Fewer than 1 mental health professional per 10,000+ people.

The Context

Across Monrovia and several counties, drug use—particularly substances like kush—has surged. Treatment centers report rising numbers of users, and the effects are devastating: petty crime, school dropouts, and family breakdown. For many young people struggling with addiction, there is simply nowhere to go and no structured path back.

The BDI Community-Rooted Response

Community Outreach

Early identification to reach at-risk youth before addiction deepens.

Counseling & Rehab

Delivering basic rehabilitation support in partnership with local providers.

Life Skills Training

Vocational training to help recovering individuals rebuild income and purpose.

Safe Engagement Spaces

Keeping vulnerable young people connected, supported, and away from risk.

Your Donation Can Help

  • Provide direct rehabilitation and counseling support to vulnerable youth
  • Fund skills training and small start-up kits for reintegration
  • Support outreach teams working in high-risk communities
  • Expand safe, community-based recovery support systems

Take Action Today

Many young people are ready to change, but lack the means. Your investment can cover training materials or a livelihood kit.
