Behind every statistic in BDI's reports is a person - a farmer who now earns more, a girl who stayed in school, a community that now manages its own natural resources. These are their words.

"Before BDI came to our community, I was farming the same way my grandfather did. Now I understand climate-smart techniques, I have access to better seeds, and my harvest has improved. My family eats better. That is everything."

James K. — Smallholder Farmer

Sinoe County, Agriculture Program

Featured Story

One story that captures what BDI's work means on the ground.

Environment & Climate Resilience

"I never thought I would see mangrove trees growing near my village again. When BDI came and asked us to be part of the tree planting, I was skeptical. But then they trained us - not just how to plant, but why it mattered for our fishing, our food, our future. Now my children are in the climate club at school. They are teaching me things I did not know. That is what real change looks like."

Mary T. — Fish Processor and Community Member

Grand Kru County  •  Environment Program Participant  • 

Grand Kru County, Liberia

More Community Voices

Stories from across BDI's eight program areas.

Samuel W.

Cooperative Leader, Bong County

Agriculture
"

BDI did not just train us and leave. They helped us form a cooperative, link us to buyers, and followed up after the training. Three of us have now expanded from subsistence to small commercial farming. That is the BDI difference.

Bong County DEMO

Fatima K.

Secondary School Student, Montserrado

Health & Nutrition
"

Every month I used to stay home for three or four days. I was too embarrassed and did not have what I needed. After BDI's menstrual health program came to my school, everything changed. I have not missed a single day of school since. My grades improved. I feel confident.

Montserrado County

Teacher Emmanuel D.

Primary School Teacher, Sinoe County

Environment
"

The BDI climate literacy program changed how I teach. I now have materials, I have a climate club in my school, and my students are genuinely excited about the environment. One of my students presented at a county-level event. She is thirteen years old. That happened because of BDI.

Sinoe County

Agnes S.

Women Farmer, Sinoe County

Gender & Youth
"

I used to farm only to feed my children. Now I farm to grow a business. BDI helped me understand my value in the agricultural chain - as a producer, as a processor, as a seller. I am not just a farmer. I am an agripreneur. My husband now works with me instead of around me.

Sinoe County

Principal Ruth J.

School Principal, Montserrado County

Education
"

BDI did not come to our school with a one-size-fits-all program. They sat with me, they listened to our challenges, they asked what our teachers needed. Then they delivered training that actually made a difference in our classrooms. This is not how most NGOs work. BDI is different.

Montserrado County

Dr. Patrick M.

Partner Organization Representative

Partnership
"

What sets BDI apart from most local NGOs we have worked with is their commitment to evidence and accountability. When we partner with BDI, we know the data will be clean, the reporting will be honest, and the communities will have been genuinely consulted. That trust is invaluable.

Monrovia DEMO
