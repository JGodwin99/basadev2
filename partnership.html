Overview

No program survives without evidence. No evidence scales without partnerships. No partnership thrives without trust.

No organization achieves lasting development impact in isolation. BDI's ability to serve communities at scale depends on three things working together: the strength of our partnerships, the quality of our evidence, and the diversity of our funding. This program area is the institutional engine that makes all other programs possible. BDI occupies a unique position in Liberia's civil society landscape - locally led, credible, corruption-resistant, and deeply mission-driven. These qualities make us a natural partner for international NGOs, foundations, bilateral donors, and government agencies seeking to maximize the impact of their investment in Liberia. We do not chase funding. We build relationships, generate evidence, and grow partnerships that outlast any single project cycle.

BDI's completed research projects have generated actionable evidence on agricultural financing, smallholder credit access, and community-level food security across four Liberian counties. These findings have directly informed program design, donor proposals, and policy advocacy - demonstrating that research is not an academic exercise for BDI. It is a practical tool for change.

Three Pillars of This Program

Strategic Partnerships

>Building and managing MOUs with government, civil society, and international organizations. Target: 10 new partnerships per year.

Evidence and Research

Conducting participatory research studies that inform program design, advocacy, and policy. Target: 5 studies completed per year.

Resource Mobilization

Submitting grant proposals, stewarding donors, and building social enterprise income. Target: 15 proposals submitted per year.

Key Objectives

✓

Achieve a 25% annual increase in partnerships and funds mobilized.

✓

Conduct 5 high-quality research studies annually to drive evidence-based decisions.

✓

Formalize 10 new strategic partnerships per year through MOUs and agreements.

✓

Maintain 90%+ donor retention through strong stewardship and impact storytelling.
