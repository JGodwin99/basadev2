Donate Now

Make a Corporate Donation — Direct Support. Measurable Impact.

A corporate donation to BASA Development Initiatives (BDI) is a direct investment in practical solutions that improve lives across Liberia.

What Your Donation Supports

Funding is applied where it is needed most to deliver clear results.

Youth Recovery

Support for those affected by drug use.

Menstrual Health

Keeping girls in school across Liberia.

Agribusiness

Development for women and young farmers.

Resilience

Community programs in health & livelihoods.

Flexible Ways to Give

General Donation

Support priority programs where funding is most needed.

Cause-Specific

Direct your contribution to a focus area aligned with your priorities.

Project-Based

Fund a defined intervention with clear deliverables and reporting.

Matched Giving

Complement employee fundraising by matching their contributions.

How to Make a Donation

1

Contact Our Partnerships Team

Discuss your preferred contribution type and focus area.

2

Agree on Scope & Structure

We align on the amount, purpose, and reporting expectations.

3

Make the Contribution

Donations made via bank transfer or agreed channels.

4

Receive Updates

Receive documentation on fund use and results achieved.

What You Can Expect

  • Transparent financial management
  • Clear documentation of fund use
  • Regular progress updates
  • Brand visibility & impact alignment
