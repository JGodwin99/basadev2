What the Board Does

The three core responsibilities of BDI's Board.

BDI's Board is not an honorary title. Each member actively fulfills three interconnected governance responsibilities that protect the organization's integrity and amplify its impact.

Set Strategic Direction

The Board approves BDI's strategic plans, reviews performance against strategic goals, and ensures the organization's programmatic priorities remain aligned with its mission and the needs of the communities it serves. The Board approved BDI's current Strategic Plan 2025-2029.

Ensure Financial Integrity

Board members hold full fiduciary responsibility for BDI's finances. They review and approve annual budgets, oversee independent financial audits, monitor expenditure against approved plans, and ensure that donor funds are managed with complete transparency and accountability.

Hold Leadership Accountable

The Board recruits, supports, and evaluates the CEO, and holds the management team accountable for performance against key goals and indicators. Through quarterly reviews and annual performance assessments, the Board ensures that BDI's leadership delivers on the commitments made to donors, partners, and communities.

How the Board Operates

A Board that governs with purpose, not just presence.

BDI's Board operates under a set of governance principles that ensure every meeting, every decision, and every oversight action adds genuine value to the organization's mission and credibility.

Regular Scheduled Meetings

Formal board meetings held on a regular schedule with documented agendas, minutes, and action items tracked to completion.

Annual Governance Reviews

Board effectiveness is reviewed annually, with governance training provided to strengthen capacity and ensure best practices are upheld.

Conflict of Interest Policy

All board members are required to declare and manage conflicts of interest, ensuring decisions are made in the best interest of BDI and the communities it serves.

Succession Planning

Leadership continuity plans are maintained to protect institutional knowledge and ensure organizational stability through board transitions.
