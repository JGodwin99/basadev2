Our Work &
Core Programs.

BASA Development Initiatives (BDI) designs and implements community-centered programs across West Africa. We focus on creating sustainable systems that empower women, elevate youth, and build long-term economic resilience.

Explore Our Programs Support Our Mission
Strategic Approach

Locally led development built on clear community outcomes.

BDI doesn't operate from a distance. We build close, trust-based partnerships with local stakeholders to identify structural bottlenecks in livelihood security, gender equity, and climate exposure. While we take on contractual or consultancy projects that perfectly align with our mission, our core focus remains on direct, long-term program delivery.

Our Strategic Pillars

  • Economic Inclusion: Moving smallholders from subsistence to profitable agribusiness value chains.
  • Equity & Access: Dismantling systemic barriers for women, youth, and persons with disabilities.
  • Resilient Systems: Integrating climate adaptation measures directly into coastal and agricultural work.

Meaningful Contribution

Not all impact requires funding. Some requires your time.

BDI works at the frontlines of community development, supporting women, youth, and vulnerable populations. Volunteering with us means contributing to work that is grounded, relevant, and focused on real outcomes. Your input is not symbolic; it directly supports program delivery and organizational growth.

What You Gain

  • Direct exposure to real-world development
  • Application of skills in meaningful contexts
  • Membership in a credible, growing NGO
  • Contribution to measurable change
