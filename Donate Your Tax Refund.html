Turn a Refund into Real Impact

A tax refund is more than a return; it is an opportunity to restore dignity, expand opportunity, and strengthen communities.

Practical Giving

Modest amounts. Major results.

Each year, many people receive a tax refund—money returned after meeting their obligations. It is often unexpected income, presenting a simple opportunity to do something meaningful without affecting day-to-day finances. By donating part or all of your tax refund to BASA Development Initiatives (BDI), you turn that return into direct support for communities across Liberia and Africa.

This form of giving is practical and intentional. It allows you to redirect funds that were not part of your regular income into something that creates lasting value for others.

Your Refund in Action

Drug Recovery

Funding reintegration support for young people affected by drug use.

Education Access

Ensuring girls have access to menstrual products and stay in school.

Agribusiness

Enabling women and youth to build sustainable livelihoods.

Health & Resilience

Strengthening community-based solutions and local development.

Make it Count

Redirecting your refund is a straightforward way to support real change.
