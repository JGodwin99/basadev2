What the Board Does

The three core responsibilities of BDI's Board.

BDI's Board is not an honorary title. Each member actively fulfills three interconnected governance responsibilities.

Set Strategic Direction

The Board approves BDI's strategic plans, reviews performance against strategic goals, and ensures programmatic priorities remain aligned with mission.

Ensure Financial Integrity

Board members hold full fiduciary responsibility for BDI's finances, reviewing budgets, audits, and ensuring donor funds are managed transparently.

Hold Leadership Accountable

The Board recruits, supports, and evaluates the CEO, holding management accountable for performance against key goals and indicators.

How the Board Operates

A Board that governs with purpose, not just presence.

BDI's Board operates under a set of governance principles that ensure every meeting, every decision, and every oversight action adds genuine value.

Regular Scheduled Meetings

Formal board meetings held on a regular schedule with documented agendas, minutes, and action items.

Annual Governance Reviews

Board effectiveness is reviewed annually, with governance training provided to strengthen capacity.

Conflict of Interest Policy

All board members are required to declare and manage conflicts of interest.

Succession Planning

Leadership continuity plans are maintained to protect institutional knowledge.
