About Katherina

The woman behind the mission, and the mission behind the woman.

Katherina M. Hopkins is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of BASA Development Initiatives - but she will tell you that BDI is not the product of a career plan. It is the product of a conviction. A conviction that Liberians have the capacity, the creativity, and the courage to build the country they deserve. What they have often lacked are the resources, the systems, and the partners who truly believe in them.

Her journey to founding BDI began on July 14, 2019 at a restaurant in Nairobi, Kenya, where a conversation with her brother and a chance encounter with a fellow African who dismissed traditional food as "primitive" sparked a defining question: why do Africans who have seen the world so often turn away from it instead of returning to build it? From that question came the mantra "Born African, Stay African" - and from that mantra came BASA.

BASA began as an online store celebrating African artisanal goods. But as Katherina immersed herself in Liberia's development landscape, she saw something that could not be ignored: a gap between the country's needs and the capacity of its institutions to meet them. Allegations of corruption, weak financial management systems, and the untapped potential of an overwhelmingly young population demanded a different kind of response.

In June 2021, she transitioned BASA into a development initiative. By 2022, BDI was formally registered as a nonprofit NGO in Liberia. Today, under her leadership, BDI operates across eight program areas from three offices, has trained over 600 farmers, reached more than 1,600 students and teachers, planted 5,000 trees, and is building toward a $2 million annual budget by 2029. All of this on an average starting budget of just $50,000 to $60,000 per year.

Katherina leads with a philosophy she has called "inside-out development" - the belief that the most durable development is designed and delivered by the communities it is meant to serve, not for them. Every program BDI runs reflects this conviction: communities are consulted first, co-design is standard, and local ownership is the goal from day one.

She serves as the primary approval authority for BDI's institutional decisions and is the organization's principal representative to donors, partners, government, and the communities BDI serves. She holds overall accountability for the implementation of BDI's Strategic Plan 2025-2029.

Leadership Philosophy

Three principles that guide how Katherina leads BDI.

These are not management frameworks. They are the convictions Katherina carries into every community consultation, every board meeting, and every funding proposal BDI submits.

01

Development From the Inside Out

The most durable change is designed and delivered by the communities it is meant to serve. BDI never arrives with pre-packaged solutions. Every program begins with listening - because communities are partners, not beneficiaries, and they hold the most important expertise in the room.

02

Accountability as the Foundation of Trust

BDI can only grow to the scale Liberia needs if it is trusted - by donors, by partners, and by the communities it serves. This means Katherina holds BDI to the same standards of transparency, reporting, and accountability as the world's best development organizations - regardless of its current size.

03

Ambitious Vision, Honest Progress

Liberia does not need another NGO with inflated claims and modest results. BDI will pursue a $2 million annual budget by 2029, expand across all 15 counties, and grow into a credible West African development institution. But it will do so honestly - publishing what works, owning what does not, and adapting along the way.

In Her Own Words

"When we founded BASA Development Initiatives, we did so with a simple but unshakeable conviction: that Liberians have the capacity, the creativity, and the courage to build the country they deserve. What they often lack are the resources, the systems, and the partners who truly believe in them. This plan is built on your trust, and fueled by our shared belief that a Liberia without poverty is not a dream. It is a destination."

Katherina M. Hopkins — Founder & CEO, BASA Development Initiatives

Key Milestones Under Her Leadership

From a spark in Nairobi to a growing organization across Liberia.

2019
Nairobi

BASA Conceived at Seroni Restaurant, Nairobi

A single conversation sparks the "Born African, Stay African" philosophy and the idea of an African-owned, Liberian-led development organization.

2021
Pivot

BASA Transitions into a Development Initiative

Katherina pivots from a cultural enterprise to a full development NGO focused on bridging institutional gaps and empowering women and youth in Liberia.

2022
Founded

BDI Formally Registered as an NGO in Liberia

BDI opens its Monrovia headquarters, launches its first agriculture and environment programs, and begins building a track record in Sinoe County.

2024
Growth

600+ Farmers Trained, 5,000 Trees Planted, 8 Programs Active

BDI delivers measurable results on an average annual budget of just $50,000-$60,000, proving the model and establishing the credibility for the Strategic Plan 2025-2029.

2025
Strategy

Strategic Plan 2025-2029 Launched

Katherina leads the development and launch of BDI's five-year plan targeting growth to $2M annually, expansion to all 15 counties, and establishment of BDI as a West African development institution.

Under her leadership, BDI secures a shortlisting for the NaFAA/OPEC Fund fisheries program and submits a $100,000 UNICEF proposal - marking BDI's entry into multi-year institutional funding.
