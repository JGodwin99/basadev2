Payroll Giving — Simple, Consistent, High Impact

Empower your workforce to sustain life-changing programs in Liberia through small, regular contributions directly from their salary.

Easy Integration

How It Works

1

Choose Amount

Employees select a fixed monthly amount to contribute.

2

Direct Deduction

Contributions are deducted directly through your payroll system.

3

Pooled Funding

Funds are pooled and directed to high-impact BDI programs.

4

Optional Matching

Companies can match donations to double the collective impact.

Where Your Contributions Go

  • Recovery and reintegration support for youth affected by drug use.
  • Ensuring girls have access to menstrual products to stay in school.
  • Supporting women and youth building income via agribusiness.
  • Delivering community health and resilience solutions.

Why Choose Payroll Giving?

Simple

No need for repeated giving decisions or manual transactions.

Consistent

Steady funding allows BDI to plan long-term community impact.

Inclusive

Enables every staff member to contribute, regardless of income level.

High Impact

Small individual amounts add up to major social change.

What BDI Provides for Organizations

  • Clear structure for internal setup
  • Transparent financial reporting
  • Communication materials for staff engagement
  • Regular program result updates
