Legacy Giving

Leave a Gift in Your Will, Create Impact That Lasts Beyond a Lifetime

Shaping the future of dignity, opportunity, and locally led development for generations to come.

Extend Your Values

Ensure the work you care about reaches communities long into the future.

Protect Your Today

This type of giving does not affect your finances now, but secures your impact later.

Enduring Impact

Sustain solutions that strengthen livelihoods across Liberia and Africa.

Leaving a gift in your will is one of the most powerful ways to support lasting change. It allows you to extend your values beyond your lifetime, ensuring that the work you care about continues to reach people and communities who need it most. By including BASA Development Initiatives (BDI) in your will, you are helping to sustain locally led solutions that strengthen livelihoods, protect dignity, and expand opportunity.

Forms of Legacy Giving

Fixed Amount

Designate a specific cash sum (Pecuniary Bequest) to be gifted from your estate.

Percentage/Residue

Allocate a percentage of your estate (Residuary Bequest) after other wishes are met.

Specific Assets

Designate specific property, stocks, or shares to support BDI's ongoing mission.

Structured Impact

Join a system that ensures your resources serve a meaningful, long-term purpose.

Supporting the Future of Liberia

Your legacy becomes part of a structured, accountable system supporting critical areas:

  • Youth Development
  • Women's Empowerment
  • Community Health
  • Climate Resilience
  • Livelihood Security
  • Education Access

Next Steps

We recommend discussing your intentions with your legal advisor to ensure your wishes are clearly documented.

BDI is available to provide necessary organizational information to give you confidence that your contribution will be managed effectively.
