Overview

From Survival Farming To A Sustainable Agribusiness Economy.

Agriculture remains the backbone of Liberia's economy, employing an estimated 65–70% of the population. BASA Development Initiatives' Agriculture and Agribusiness Program is designed to shift from subsistence farming to farming as a business. Through climate-smart practices, value addition, and structured market engagement, BDI is increasing incomes and creating rural employment, especially for women and youth.

Key Objectives

✓

Empower women and youth in agribusiness through skills and finance.

✓

Increase productivity with climate-smart, market-oriented farming.

✓

Expand access to quality seeds, tools, and post-harvest tech.

✓

Strengthen value chains linking production, processing & markets.
