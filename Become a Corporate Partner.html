Partnership Overview

Businesses in Liberia and globally are looking beyond profit to impact.

Communities are changing, expectations are rising, and companies are being called to play a more active role in addressing real social challenges. This is not just about corporate responsibility; it is about the long-term sustainability of markets, workforces, and the environments in which businesses operate.

BASA Development Initiatives (BDI) offers a practical way for companies to do this through structured, credible partnerships that deliver measurable results. We work at the intersection of economic opportunity, social protection, and community development, supporting women, youth, and vulnerable populations across sectors, including agribusiness, fisheries, climate resilience, education, and public health.

Flexible Partnership Models

Strategic Programs

Co-invest in youth employment, women’s empowerment, or climate-smart agriculture with clear visibility.

Social Investment (CSI)

Direct funding toward high-impact initiatives that align perfectly with your brand and corporate values.

Employee Engagement

Opportunities for staff volunteering, mentorship, and skills-sharing that strengthen teams.

Key Benefits for Partners

Brand Alignment: Credible, community-based impact visibility.

Stakeholder Engagement: Meaningful interaction with beneficiaries.

Market Stability: Contributing to a more stable, skilled population.

In-Kind Support: Contribute expertise or equipment to our delivery.
