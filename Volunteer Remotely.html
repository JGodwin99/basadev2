Volunteer With BDI — Use Your Skills Where They Matter

Impact doesn’t require a plane ticket. Bring your time, skills, and networks to the table and play a direct role in strengthening Liberia’s future.

Meaningful Contribution

Not all impact requires funding. Some requires your time.

BDI works at the frontlines of community development, supporting women, youth, and vulnerable populations. Volunteering with us means contributing to work that is grounded, relevant, and focused on real outcomes. Your input is not symbolic; it directly supports program delivery and organizational growth.

What You Gain

  • Direct exposure to real-world development
  • Application of skills in meaningful contexts
  • Membership in a credible, growing NGO
  • Contribution to measurable change
Priority Opportunities

Where You Can Contribute

BDI is currently seeking remote volunteers with specific technical expertise to help expand our reach and strategic direction.

Marketing & Branding

Help us tell our story and raise visibility for Liberia’s most pressing community needs.

Grant & Proposal Writing

Contribute technical input to strengthen our funding pipelines and expand program reach.

Advisory & Governance

Provide board-level expertise to improve our strategic direction and organizational systems.

The BDI Volunteer Profile

What You Bring to the Team

Professional Expertise

Relevant professional skills in your chosen area of contribution.

Consistent Commitment

A willingness to commit time consistently to meet project milestones.

Results-Oriented

A practical approach focused on delivering sustainable, local impact.

Local Alignment

A commitment to BDI’s focus on locally led development and systems.
